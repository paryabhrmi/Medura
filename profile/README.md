## Hey there, I'm Parya 👋

**Product Designer · Design Technologist · Design Systems & AI-Native Interfaces**

I design interfaces and then build them. My background is a software engineering
degree that turned into a product design practice, which means I spend most of my
time in the gap between those two disciplines — the part where a mockup meets a
browser and one of them has to give.

Over the past few years I've worked on SaaS dashboards, B2B agency tooling, ERP
panels, and 0→1 product architecture: the kind of systems where five different
roles touch the same data and every one of them needs a different view of it. I
build the design system, standardise the handoff, sit with the engineers while it
ships, and stay accountable for whether the thing that shipped still feels like
the thing that was designed.

Lately I've been working on AI-native product surfaces — human-in-the-loop
review flows, agent experience patterns, and features I prototype and implement
myself rather than describe in a spec. Alongside that: motion built in Rive,
accessibility held to WCAG 2.2, and teaching designers the engineering side of
the workflow.

I care about design systems that survive contact with a codebase, specifications
written in numbers instead of adjectives, and documentation a teammate can
actually follow. If you're working on complex product surfaces, design
infrastructure, or the tooling between design and engineering, I'd like to hear
about it.

You can find more of my work at **[paryabahrami.ir](https://paryabahrami.ir)**.

## What I Focus On

- **Design Systems That Survive Implementation**
  Architecting, building and maintaining component libraries with tokens and
  variables that map cleanly onto the front-end framework the team actually uses
  — not a library that looks right in Figma and falls apart in code.

- **Design-to-Code Handoff and DesignOps**
  Standardising the path from design file to shipped component: naming, theming,
  token sync, review cadence, and delivery specs engineers don't have to
  interpret.

- **Complex, Multi-Role Product Flows**
  Designing SaaS, B2B and ERP surfaces where consumer users, partner agencies and
  internal operations teams all work against the same system and each need a
  different truth from it.

- **AI-Native Interaction Design**
  Human-in-the-loop review, agent experience patterns, and AI features designed
  around what the model gets wrong as much as what it gets right — prototyped and
  implemented, not just specified.

- **Motion and Interaction Fidelity**
  Interaction and motion built in Rive and shipped to real runtimes, where the
  easing curve, the frame budget and the reduced-motion path are all part of the
  design, not a polish pass.

- **Research and Product Analytics**
  Standing up ResearchOps on Dovetail, Mixpanel and Clarity so decisions get
  validated against evidence, and JTBD and segmentation work that tells you which
  three problems out of twelve are worth solving.

- **Accessibility as a Baseline**
  WCAG 2.2 conformance, keyboard paths, focus states and reduced-motion treated
  as part of the definition of done rather than a remediation ticket.

- **Teaching the Engineering Half of Design**
  Writing and tooling that gets designers comfortable with the parts of the
  workflow they were told weren't theirs — version control being the first one.
